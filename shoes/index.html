Questions

Good to know

How do I pay?

Once you send your order on WhatsApp, we confirm availability and share payment details. Your pair ships as soon as payment is confirmed.

How long does delivery take?

Within the city, 24–48 hours. Other states, 2–4 working days. Delivery fees depend on your location and are confirmed before you pay.

What if my size doesn't fit?

Message us within 48 hours of delivery. Unworn pairs in their original box can be exchanged for another size, subject to stock.

How do I find my size?

Measure your foot from heel to longest toe in cm and send it to us. As a guide: 23.5cm ≈ EU 37, 24cm ≈ EU 38, 24.5cm ≈ EU 39, 25cm ≈ EU 40.

Can't find your size or colour? Just ask.

New pairs arrive often. Tell us what you're looking for and we'll let you know the moment it lands.
